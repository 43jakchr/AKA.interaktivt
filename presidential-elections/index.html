Interactive task

Presidential Elections

Arrange the events in the correct order based on the information given in the video. Drag the cards to reorder them, or use the arrow buttons. Click the speech bubbles to add more details, such as the three basic requirements for becoming president and when Inauguration Day takes place.

Tip: On mobile, you can drag the left-hand handles or use the up and down buttons. Your notes stay attached to their cards as you reorder them.
